# Engineering Provenance

XMECK-AI preserves the engineering path that led to the current product instead of rewriting its origin.

```text

        |
        v
QBOX-AI Stage B R2
Founder-accepted working local-intelligence milestone
        |
        v
XMECK-AI 1.0.0.0

```

## What this demonstrates

The public lineage exposes evidence of:
- iterative Windows product construction;
- local-model/runtime integration;
- explicit model admission and readiness;
- qualification and evidence discipline;
- project/Git and Knowledge evolution;
- bounded authority / ProjectGuard;
- packaging, recovery and lifecycle engineering;
- SBOM/provenance/manifest thinking;
- explicit claim boundaries.

## What it does not establish

The repository does not by itself establish:
- commercial traction;
- product-market fit;
- revenue or retention;
- independent security certification;
- category leadership;
- global novelty or patentability.

Those require separate evidence.
